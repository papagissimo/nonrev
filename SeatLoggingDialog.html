Yardstickⓘ - how big a move matters, by current can-buy total
total move, total move, ...
Each pair is a can-buy total (all cabins summed) and the move, up or down, that would matter from there. Totals between pairs are drawn smoothly; past either end the nearest pair holds. The yardstick works both ways: from 3 a drop to 0 changes nothing, but a climb to 6 is a white knuckle turning into a seat.

A reading has "a move that matters still to come" when it sits farther from that day's golden-ticket total than the yardstick allows at the reading's own total. Each pool's curve is the share of its flights in that state at each distance before departure: a flat, low curve means one reading anywhere tells you nearly everything; a curve that falls means later readings catch moves the earlier ones couldn't.

Changing the yardstick regrows the pools.
Now! atⓘ points   Skip under points
Points are what a reading now would buy: the pool's curve at your last reading's distance, less the curve now. If the last look was at T-24 with 40% of the pool still to move, and now at T-12 it's 28%, a reading now catches 12 points' worth of flights.

At or above Now! the row turns green. Between the two it's light gray, Meh, if you must. Under Skip it's dark gray: skip this, dammit, you've got better things to do. A reading an hour after the last buys almost nothing, so it lands in Skip on its own.

Two rows ignore the points: a flight with no reading yet (the leadoff) and a flight inside the golden-ticket window not yet read there are always Now!, because the study needs both readings whatever the curve says. Once the golden ticket is in, the flight is Skip.

Under each row's hours, next rdg is when it next turns Now!, on this device's clock, rounded up to 15 minutes.
Away marginⓘ h
With Back at set, every flight departing before you're back plus this margin is Last: the reading now is the last one it will get, so make it a full count. The margin is working room to get through the list once you're back. Last rows come first: never-read flights, then by points, then no pool. Back at clears itself once that time passes.
